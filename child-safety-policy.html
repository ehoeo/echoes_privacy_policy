01

Our Commitment to Child Safety

Echoes and its developer, Surag VC, maintain a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to providing a safe digital environment and strictly prohibit any content, behavior, or interaction that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

Any user found engaging in conduct that endangers or exploits a child will be permanently removed from the platform and reported to relevant authorities without exception.

02

Age Restriction

Echoes is restricted to users aged 16 years and older. This applies globally regardless of local age-of-majority laws.

Accounts identified as belonging to individuals under the required age limit will be immediately and permanently terminated, and any associated data will be deleted in accordance with our data retention policy.

03

Prohibited Content and Behavior

While Echoes is designed with privacy in mind — meaning users do not upload personal images and cannot view the public profiles of others — we actively prohibit any form of exploitation through text, audio, or external links. The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

CSAM

Sharing or linking to any visual depiction of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct (Child Sexual Abuse Material).

Grooming

Befriending or attempting to build a relationship with a minor for the purpose of sexual contact or exploitation.

Sextortion

Threatening or blackmailing a minor using real or alleged access to intimate imagery.

Trafficking

Advertising or soliciting a minor for commercial sexual exploitation of any kind.

This list is not exhaustive. Any behavior that endangers the safety or wellbeing of a child — whether or not explicitly listed — is prohibited and subject to the same enforcement actions.

04

In-App Feedback and Reporting Mechanism

User safety is our highest priority. If you encounter any suspicious behavior, inappropriate content, or suspected CSAE activity within Echoes, we urge you to report it immediately.

Users can submit reports through:

  • The in-app reporting tools available within the Echoes application
  • Directly contacting our dedicated safety team by email (see Section 6 below)

All reports are treated with strict confidentiality and reviewed by our safety team promptly. We do not retaliate against users for good-faith reports.

05

Enforcement and Legal Compliance

We take immediate and decisive action against any user found violating our Child Safety Standards. Confirmed or suspected violations trigger the following response:

1
Immediate Account Termination

The offending user's account is permanently suspended without appeal.

2
Evidence Preservation

Relevant data is preserved in accordance with applicable law to support any investigation.

3
Authority Reporting

Cases are reported immediately to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or appropriate regional law enforcement agencies.
